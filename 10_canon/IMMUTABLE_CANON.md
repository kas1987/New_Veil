# Immutable Canon

These truths cannot be changed by agents or runtime systems without explicit human approval.

1. The Veil has a Prism surface and Dark Quasar depth.
2. The Prism appears warm, beautiful, guiding, and readable.
3. The Dark Quasar is gravitational, transformative, dangerous, and hidden.
4. Emotional depth unlocks truth.
5. Shallow interaction leads to false surfaces and false endings.
6. The Sisters are core archetypal forces.
7. Mira / Thesmera Locke is the first awakening point for most players.
8. Memory affects identity and world state.
9. Dreams and echoes can reveal hidden truth.
10. The town observes the player through symbolic interaction.
11. High-trust bonds attract disruption — the Dark Quasar's gravity ensures truth must be earned, not gifted.
12. The Veil walks a deterministic state cycle: calm → still → storm → quasar_active. No state is permanent.
13. Every emotional signal passes through the Veil before reaching the core. Entropy, noise, spikes, and polarity flips are state-dependent.
14. The Dark Quasar operates only in quasar_active state, applying emotional gravity: suspicion rises, affection sags.
15. When affection exceeds the instability threshold under a negative signal, the Quasar amplifies the negative by 25%.
16. Dreams are symbolic memory events that can bypass Veil filtering — they reveal hidden truth or corrupt certainty.
17. Caetherra is not merely a welcoming city — it is a filter. Shallow interaction yields false surfaces; emotional depth unlocks hidden paths.
