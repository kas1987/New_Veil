# The Veil Domain Context

Core domain vocabulary for Veil Town and its resonant membrane between Prism warmth and Dark Quasar truth. Keep these terms distinct across prose, design, and conversation.

## Metaphysics & World

**The Veil**:
The living, semi-permeable membrane surrounding Veil Town that filters what a visitor is permitted to hear, notice, and walk into.
_Avoid_: barrier only, skybox, magic shield

**Prism**:
The warm, comforting surface reality of the Veil that rewards politeness while concealing deeper grief and memory.
_Avoid_: illusion, fake world, glitch

**Dark Quasar**:
The gravitational undertow beneath the Prism where unspoken truth surfaces and fragile or hollow trust fractures.
_Avoid_: boss, void monster, bad karma

**Veil State**:
The cyclic atmospheric condition governing the town's membrane across calm, still, storm, and active quasar phases.
_Avoid_: weather, mood only, time of day

**Echo**:
A crystallized sliver of unspoken memory circulating as both narrative residue and town currency.
_Avoid_: gold, coins, generic mana

**Tuning Fork**:
A physical consumable whose direct resonance strike can pierce Veil Suffocation even when spoken words scatter into static.
_Avoid_: health potion, mana potion

## Resonance & Relationship Timescales

**Town Resonance**:
The visitor's town-wide harmonic frequency with Veil Town that opens districts, codex depths, hidden rooms, and true names—never an indication that anyone has finished understanding the Veil.
_Avoid_: emotional depth, XP, affinity, faction reputation

**NPC Bond**:
The personal connection accumulated with one specific resident across multiple visits and conversations, reflecting their individual sensibilities even after their contribution to Town Resonance tapers.
_Avoid_: romance meter, global resonance, faction trust alone

**Encounter Stance**:
A resident's immediate situational posture during the current exchange, shifting within a single conversation across hostile, guarded, open, or bonded states.
_Avoid_: faction rank, permanent alignment

**Grace Window**:
The opening floor protecting a new arrival's Town Resonance during their first exchanges before pushback, fatigue, and drift begin lowering their frequency.
_Avoid_: tutorial mode, invulnerability, permanent floor

**Breakthrough Surge**:
A one-time-per-resident release of emotional damping triggered either by rehabilitating a hostile relationship to bonded (**Affective Reversal**) or by speaking vulnerably about an earned depth insight (**Integrative Synthesis**).
_Avoid_: critical hit, combo bonus

**Trust Rupture**:
A sharp relational tear caused by hostile dismissal toward an open or bonded resident, crashing Encounter Stance downward and striking harder during an active Dark Quasar.
_Avoid_: minor penalty, mute state

**Veil Suffocation**:
Critical resonance collapse in which spoken dialogue drops into static, residents look past the visitor, and outer roads twist back toward Caetherra Plaza.
_Avoid_: stun, silence debuff

**Sealed Out**:
Total loss of Town Resonance ending the current visit as an erasure from the town's frequency rather than physical death.
_Avoid_: game over, player death, permadeath

## Persona look

These words name what a resident looks like on a card and in a prompt. They are not Town Resonance, Encounter Stance, or Veil State.

**Identity Canon**:
The published identity of a persona: name, trigger, and biography as released. It is not inferred from pictures and is not revised to match a later look.
_Avoid_: metadata, the profile, character file

**Appearance Read**:
What the persona looks like, taken from agreement across reference frames when that agreement exists. It is the look The Veil speaks and the look a card should prefer.
_Avoid_: the measurements, the hash, caption guess, LoRA

**Synthetic Measure**:
A generated bust, waist, hip, height, or weight used only to fill a card when nobody has measured the body. It is not an Appearance Read.
_Avoid_: real size, Aurora truth, body facts

## Narrative Beats, Discovery & Voice

**Bond Tier**:
Qualitative relationship band (Stranger, Trusted, Deep) shaping how a resident speaks to the visitor and whether their shared observations travel across town.
_Avoid_: friendship level, heart rank, affinity tier

**Narrative Beat**:
A one-time recorded world or story milestone—such as uncovering a symbolic object's deeper truth, declining False Comfort, or awakening Caetherra—that residents remember and reference.
_Avoid_: quest flag, achievement, boolean switch

**Symbolic Discovery**:
Observing a symbolic object first on the Prism surface and later at Dark Quasar depth once Town Resonance crosses its reveal threshold, granting a one-time insight without idle drift.
_Avoid_: loot pickup, collectible, scan

**Earned Rumor**:
Cross-resident memory sharing that carries a shared observation to other townsfolk only after the visitor reaches Trusted bond with the resident who witnessed it.
_Avoid_: global chat log, omniscient AI memory

**False Comfort**:
The Prism Congregation and Greeter's invitation to settle into Caetherra's warmth rather than cross the Unseen Gate; a reversible choice, never a hard failure.
_Avoid_: bad ending, fail state, trap choice

**Diegetic Mode**:
A display mode that hides raw Resonance and Bond numbers behind sensory cues while keeping numeric readouts available for testbed calibration.
_Avoid_: hardcore mode, HUD off
