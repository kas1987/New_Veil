# Veil States

The Veil cycles through named emotional fields. Each state reshapes how signals
flow through the simulation — distorting perception, amplifying danger, or
offering brief calm before the next transformation.

## The Cycle

The Veil does not choose its state randomly. It walks a deterministic schedule:

`calm → still → storm → quasar_active → still → calm → storm → quasar_active`

This cycle repeats forever. No state is permanent. No safety lasts.

## State Descriptions

### Calm
The Prism surface at its gentlest. Signals flow nearly undistorted. Entropy
multiplier is 0.85× — meaning events are slightly softened. Spike probability is
only 2%. Trust must reach 100 for any quasar logic.

In Calm, the town is readable. Mira's warmth is genuine. The Dark Quasar sleeps.
But Calm never lasts.

### Still
The membrane holds steady. Entropy is 1.0× — signals pass at face value.
Noise at 0.10 std-dev introduces gentle distortion. 5% spike chance.
Trust threshold drops to 80.

Still is the space between. Not dangerous, not safe. The town watches.
Nothing reveals itself, but nothing hides. A waiting state.

### Storm
The surface fractures. Entropy surges to 1.6×. Noise at 0.25 — one quarter
of every signal is distorted. 12% spike probability with 1.5× intensity.
Flip chance reaches 20%. Trust threshold drops to 70.

In Storm, emotions intensify. Mira becomes harder to read. Relationships
that seemed stable waver. The Prism flickers. The Veil's membrane
thins, and what lies beneath begins to show through.

### Quasar Active
The Veil opens. The Dark Quasar is **active** — this is the only state where
emotional gravity applies. Entropy at 1.4×, noise at 0.20, spike probability
at 18%, intensity at 1.8×, flip chance at 30%. Trust threshold collapses to 55.

In Quasar Active, the "no easy wins" mechanic activates. High-trust, high-affection
relationships attract disruption. Suspicion rises. Affection sags. Negative
signals amplify 25% when affection exceeds the instability threshold.

This is the transformative state. Certainty breaks. Truth forces itself through
the cracks. Those who emerge from Quasar Active with trust intact have earned it.

## Fictional Framing

The cycle is not random because truth is not random. The Veil follows a rhythm:
calm invitation, still observation, storm challenge, quasar transformation.
Every player who enters Caetherra will walk this cycle whether they know it or not.

The question is never *whether* the Quasar will test you. The question is whether
you will recognize the test when it comes — and whether your trust was earned
deeply enough to survive the gravity of truth.
