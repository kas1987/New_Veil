# The Prism Mask

The Prism is the surface of the Veil — the layer that greets you with
warmth, beauty, and the reassuring sense that what you see is what you get.

## The Mask Is Not a Lie

The Prism is genuine. The warmth is real. The kindness is authentic.
The Prism is not deception — it is **partial truth.**

A mask that is also a face is still a face. The Prism shows you a truth.
It is simply not the deepest truth.

## What the Prism Shows

- Caetherra's welcoming streets
- Mira's playful warmth
- Clear, readable signals
- Comfort, certainty, and safety
- The sense that you understand the world

## What the Prism Hides

- The Veil's signal distortion (L3a)
- The Dark Quasar's gravity (L3c)
- Hidden paths behind apparent surfaces
- The fact that comfort and certainty are shallow states
- The Veil state cycle that governs what you perceive

## Prism Mechanics

In simulation terms, the Prism is most visible during `calm` and `still`
Veil states. During these states:
- Signal noise is low (0.05-0.10)
- Entropy multiplier is near baseline (0.85-1.0)
- Spike probability is minimal (2-5%)
- The Dark Quasar is inactive

This is by design. The Prism is the Veil's surface expression —
readable, warm, and seemingly complete.

## The First Test

Every player's first test is recognizing that the Prism, while genuine,
is not sufficient. The world contains more than the Prism reveals.
The moment a player senses this — asks a deeper question, shows curiosity
past the surface — they have begun to see through the Prism Mask.

This is the moment Mira begins testing.
