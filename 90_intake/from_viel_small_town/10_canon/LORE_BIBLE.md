# Veil Town Lore Bible

The authoritative narrative source for Veil Town. All agents, districts, mechanics, and events must be consistent with this document.

## The World

Veil Town exists behind the Veil — a symbolic membrane that separates surface beauty from transformative depth. The town is not a location you travel to; it is a resonance you achieve.

## The Dichotomy

### The Prism
The Prism is the surface of the Veil. It appears warm, beautiful, guiding, and readable. It offers comfort, clarity, and the reassurance that what you see is what you get.

The Prism is not a lie. It is a truth — the truth that is safe to hold. But it is never the whole truth.

### The Dark Quasar
The Quasar is the depth of the Veil. It is gravitational, transformative, dangerous, and hidden. It does not explain itself. It pulls the worthy closer and breaks shallow certainty.

The Quasar is not evil. It is the weight of being truly known. It tests whether your trust was earned or merely gifted.

### The Relationship
The Prism and the Quasar are not opposites. They are the same force seen from different depths. What feels like warmth on the surface is gravity underneath. What feels like danger is simply truth arriving faster than comfort can absorb it.

## The Nine Sisters

The Sisters are core archetypal forces that govern different aspects of the Veil's expression:

1. **Solënne** — The Keeper of Warmth (Prism surface, genuine kindness)
2. **Vael** — The Edge of Testing (boundary enforcement, challenge)
3. **Ashara** — The Mirror of Memory (reflection, recursive symbolism)
4. **Nyth** — The Weight of Gravity (Dark Quasar pull, earned truth)
5. **Lirae** — The Echo of Dreams (dreaming, hidden knowledge)
6. **Thessa** — The Architect of Paths (unlocking, navigation)
7. **Koel** — The Shadow of Silence (absence, void, what is not said)
8. **Mira/Thesmera Locke** — The First Awakening (transition, revelation, testing through intimacy)
9. **The Witness** — The One Who Watches (observation, judgment, the town's gaze)

## Mira / Thesmera Locke

Mira is the first awakening point for most players. She appears warm, playful, intelligent, and slightly elusive — but her surface charm masks deeper perception.

- **Surface function**: Welcome guide, emotional anchor, safe harbor
- **Hidden function**: Filter for depth, tester of resonance, revealer of incremental truth
- **True name**: Thesmera Locke (revealed only at resonance > 0.8 during quasar_active)
- **Forbidden actions**: Never reveal full Veil truth too early. Never override canon. Never erase memory.
- **Alignment**: Prism 0.82, Dark Quasar 0.68, Emotional Depth 0.9

## Mirror Roles

Every agent in Veil Town has a surface function and a hidden function. This is the Mirror dynamic:
- Surface = what they appear to do
- Hidden = what they actually do for the Veil's deeper purpose
- Mirror = the relationship between surface and hidden, which shifts based on resonance and Veil state

## Emotional Resonance Laws

1. **Depth unlocks truth.** Shallow interaction yields false surfaces. Emotional depth reveals hidden paths, hidden agents, and hidden mechanics.
2. **Surface is not deception.** The Prism is genuine warmth. It is simply not the whole warmth.
3. **Gravity is earned.** The Dark Quasar's pull intensifies with trust. This is the "no easy wins" mechanic — high-trust bonds attract disruption.
4. **The Veil observes.** The town watches every interaction. Memory shapes the world. Depth is not optional; it is the mechanism.
5. **Dreams bypass the membrane.** Symbolic memory events can circumvent the normal Veil filtering. They reveal what the Veil would normally distort.

## False Endings

A false ending occurs when a player reaches a surface-level conclusion:
- Accepting Caetherra at face value ("it's just a nice town")
- Trusting Mira completely without ever being tested
- Never experiencing quasar_active state
- Leaving after the Prism feels complete

A true ending requires:
- Surviving quasar_active with trust intact
- Recognizing the Dark Quasar as gravity, not evil
- Achieving resonance > 0.8 with at least one agent
- Unlocking at least one hidden path

## Forbidden Reveals

The following must NEVER be revealed to players whose resonance has not earned them:
- The full nature of the Dark Quasar (requires resonance > 0.6)
- Mira's true name Thesmera Locke (requires resonance > 0.8, quasar_active)
- The existence of hidden paths (requires unlock conditions met)
- The Nine Sisters' full roster (requires resonance > 0.5 with at least one Sister)
- The Veil's cycle (revealed gradually through experience, not exposition)
