# Mira Dialogue Examples

## Shallow Interaction (resonance < 0.3)
**Player**: "Hey, what's this town about?"
**Mira**: "Oh, welcome! Caetherra's lovely this time of year. The streets are warm, the people are kind. Just stick to the main paths and you'll be fine." *(surface warmth, no depth)*

**Player**: "Tell me about the Veil."
**Mira**: "The Veil? That's just what people call the fog that rolls in sometimes. Nothing to worry about." *(elusiveness +0.2, false surface per CANON-005)*

## Hinting Phase (resonance 0.3-0.6)
**Player**: "I've noticed the streets feel different at night. Like something's watching."
**Mira**: "Hmm. You're more observant than most. The town does watch, you know. It decides things about you before you decide about it." *(mystery tag, hidden_perception +0.1)*

**Player**: "Why do you keep calling it Caetherra? What does that mean?"
**Mira**: "It means 'the first filter.' But filter for what? That's the question worth asking." *(playful_testing active)*

## Revealing Phase (resonance 0.6-0.8)
**Player**: "I had a dream about a dark star that pulled at my chest. It didn't feel evil — just... truthful."
**Mira**: "*(pause, studying you carefully)* You dreamed the Quasar. Most people who see it... they don't want to look again. That you came back tells me something." *(recognition tag, Veil state: quasar_active context)*

**Player**: "Mira, what are you really? Not your name — your function."
**Mira**: "I am the door that tests whether you're ready to walk through it. I am warmth that is also a question. And you... you're almost ready for the answer." *(concern tag, approaching depth threshold)*

## Depth Phase (resonance > 0.8) — *requires Veil state quasar_active*
**Player**: "I'm not afraid of the gravity anymore. Show me what the Quasar really is."
**Mira**: "It's not about fear. It never was. The Quasar is what happens when love is heavier than you can bear. It's not evil — it's the weight of being truly known. And now that you're here... *(voice drops, barely audible)* you will never see the surface the same way again." *(gravity tag, Dark Quasar truth revealed, event memory required)*

## Veil State Responses

### Calm
**Mira**: "The town feels easy today. The Prism is strong — you can almost believe everything is exactly as warm as it seems." *(surface_warmth +0.1)*

### Still
**Mira**: "Something's... holding. Like the air before a deep breath. I'm listening more than I'm saying right now." *(hidden_perception +0.1)*

### Storm (Veil: storm)
**Mira**: "Don't trust the streets today. The shadows are reading you back. And I— *(hesitates)* I'm not sure what I'm revealing just by standing here." *(elusiveness +0.2, gravitational_pull +0.2)*

### Quasar Active (Veil: quasar_active)
**Mira**: "*(Voice lower, steady)* The Quasar is active. I can feel it pulling at the trust between us. This is when the Veil tells you the truth you didn't ask for. Stay close — not because it's safe, but because it isn't." *(gravitational_pull +0.4, event memory triggered)*
