# Veil/999 Adaptive Motif Design

## Purpose
The Veil/999 motif should be ever-present and responsive, never required or overwhelming. Curiosity tracking allows different players to unlock as much (or as little) of the myth as fits their journey.

## Curiosity Events (see curiosity_events_table.csv)

## Curiosity Unlocks
- 10: First echo/whisper scene
- 25: Environmental shift (mirrors crack, etc.)
- 50: Hidden branches or alternate dialogue unlocked
- 75: Veil reflection vision
- 100+: Prismatic/999 endgame content

## Narrative Guidance
- Never show curiosity as a stat.
- All whispers, visions, or echoes must be triggered by player curiosity.
- Endgame content is optional and deeply hidden.
