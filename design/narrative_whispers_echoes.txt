# Veil Whispers & Echoes

## Low Curiosity
"You brush dust from a half-broken mirror. In its dim reflection, your eyes seem deeper than before. For a moment, you sense someone watching—a shape that vanishes when you blink."

## Mid Curiosity
"That familiar hallway feels warped tonight. Every surface shines too brightly, shadows flicker with shapes. A line of cracked glass trails behind you, but when you turn, it’s gone."

## High Curiosity
“Most turn back here,” whispers the voice in the glass. “But you… you remember. Or perhaps you are only dreaming?”

## Very High Curiosity
"In a lucid dream, you stand before an infinite hall of mirrors. Every version of yourself is searching, but none look away from the Veil. When you reach out, your hand passes through her face—warm, unfinished, perfect in its imperfection."
